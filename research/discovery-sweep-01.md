## The organizing find

The index is missing its **historical spine**. Every current entry is a crypto-era or frontier-era agent. The most durable cultural agents — the ones with a *decades-long* body of work, which is exactly what a diachronic benchmark is built to reward — are absent. Adding them is not just corpus growth; it is what makes the "4th stage / measures durability" claim credible. A durability benchmark with no pre-2020 entries reads as a crypto index wearing a culture costume.
